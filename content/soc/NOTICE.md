## What was used and what changed

- **Taken from SOC-CMM:** the 5 domain and 27 aspect names (lightly reworded, for example "Customers and
  stakeholders" for "Customers & Stakeholders"), the maturity level names (0 Non-existent to 5 Optimising, written
  in Australian English) and capability level names (0 Incomplete to 3 Defined), and the default targets (maturity 3,
  capability 2).
- **Written for crownguard:** every question, "why it matters" note and level description. They are not SOC-CMM
  questions or guidance text, and they are not a subset of the SOC-CMM questionnaire.
- **Simplified scoring:** one 0–5 maturity rating per aspect, plus one 0–3 capability rating for each technology and
  service aspect, instead of SOC-CMM's hundreds of scored questions. Aspect and domain scores are unweighted means,
  as in SOC-CMM's basic tool.

Results are indicative self-ratings. They are not SOC-CMM maturity or capability scores and are not comparable with
a full SOC-CMM assessment, the SOC Maturity Report benchmark or SOC-CMM certification.
